From the fall of 2024 to my graduation in May 2026, I developed a Python package for performing specialized molecular dynamics calculations. The idea was to allow other researchers to perform the same types of analysis described [here](/2026/09/28/REU-work.html) in their own areas of interest. This project involved writing and testing a Python package, learning Slurm and HPC scheduling, and communicating with scientific experts to translate their domain understanding into software. Check out the repo [here](https://github.com/Ibrahimsuf/fluctuation-theory-thompson-group).
